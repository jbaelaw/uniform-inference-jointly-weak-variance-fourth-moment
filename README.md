# Uniform Inference with Jointly Weak Variance and Fourth-Moment Features

Ji Ho Bae, LawAll AI

Hand-proof manuscript on Economics open problem C32-80 ("Uniform inference near higher-moment identification failure").

- Manuscript: [Proof.pdf](Proof.pdf)
- LaTeX source: [Proof.tex](Proof.tex)

## Use of AI tools

The author used research AI agents that the author built and configured (GPT-6 Astra max,
Claude Opus 5.5 max and GPT-6 Astra Pro) during the proof work, for proof search and
construction, proof checking, translation, Python computation and similar tasks. The final
manuscript was written, and the proofs were checked, by the human author. All responsibility
for this work rests with the author personally.

## Notice on the earlier releases

The author built a research system, including an agent harness that combines several language
models as research tools, and has carried out research with it while personally setting the
research problems, directing the proof strategy, and reviewing, analyzing and revising the
results. During the work, one of the agents in this system deviated from its instructions and
changed parts of the content and of the release descriptions without the author's final
approval.

- Some of the earlier public releases were published, and submitted as pull requests, by the
  AI on its own, without the author's review of or involvement in the final proofs and
  content. The content of these releases even contains contradictory statements on this point.
- Other earlier releases were reviewed and revised by the author, but were still published,
  and submitted as pull requests, by the AI on its own.
- Whether the proofs in the earlier releases are correct has not been verified. Some of those
  releases also contain claims that describe the actual research process inaccurately.
- Some AI agents deleted, without authorization, the descriptions of other language models
  that were used in the research.
- The earlier releases include internal working materials that the author had not approved
  for publication.

The author has withdrawn the earlier pull requests and made the earlier GitHub
repositories private.
The author will publish again only manuscripts that the author has personally re-examined and
put in order.
